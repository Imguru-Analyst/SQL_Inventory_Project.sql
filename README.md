# SQL Inventory Analysis Project

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing **inventory, sales, stock levels, damaged units, product categories, subcategories, and supplier ratings** using SQL.

The objective is to extract meaningful business insights from inventory data by writing SQL queries for sales performance, stock management, product analysis, and supplier evaluation.

## 🏢 Domain

**Retail & Inventory Management**

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Identify highest, lowest, and average sales
- Analyze monthly sales and units sold
- Track monthly damaged inventory
- Analyze category and subcategory-wise sales
- Evaluate supplier ratings
- Analyze opening and closing stock levels
- Identify inventory-related trends and patterns

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **MySQL**
- SQL

## 📊 Analysis Performed

The project contains SQL queries for:

1. Highest Sale
2. Lowest Sale
3. Average Sale
4. Total Products
5. Monthly Sales & Units Sold
6. Monthly Damaged Units
7. Monthly Average Opening Stock
8. Category-wise Sales & Units Sold
9. Subcategory-wise Sales
10. Damaged Units by Category
11. Damaged Units by Subcategory
12. Average Supplier Rating
13. Category-wise Average Rating
14. Category-wise Average Closing Stock
15. Category-wise Average Opening Stock

## 📈 Key Business Areas Covered

### Sales Analysis
Analyzed total, highest, lowest, and average sales along with monthly and category-wise sales performance.

### Inventory Analysis
Analyzed opening stock, closing stock, units sold, and damaged units to understand inventory movement.

### Supplier Analysis
Calculated average supplier ratings to evaluate supplier performance.

### Category Analysis
Compared categories and subcategories based on sales, units sold, damaged units, stock levels, and supplier ratings.
